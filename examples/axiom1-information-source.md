# Axiom I through the toolbox — "Information as the source of geometry"

Demonstration of the `reframe-through-structure` skill over the `lat` catalogue,
run against a real source text (a physics working paper, *Information als Quelle
der Raumzeit*, Axiom I — the paper is not bundled in this repository).
An apt target: *"information as the **source** of geometry"* is the genealogical
tendency the founding essay warns against, in pure form — spacetime is handed an
ancestor.

**Input language:** German → `exclude_names: ["German", "English"]` (baseline
pair). Forms are language-neutral.

**Source (Axiom I).** [Setzung] an entropy-density quantity I_µν as a *source* in
the field equation G_µν + Λg_µν = κℏω I_µν; [folgt] ∇^µ I_µν = 0; [etabliert]
gravity as emergent from information (Jacobson, Verlinde); [offen] the form of
L_info is not derived.

## 1 · Diagnose the bias

- **"source" / "emergent"** install a one-way arrow: information = origin/cause,
  geometry = descendant/effect. This *is* the genealogical tendency (everything
  gets an ancestor) → axes **Causality** + **Agency & rank**.
- But the field equation is a **bilateral "="** — historically read both ways
  (matter ↔ curvature). "source" injects an arrow the equals sign does not carry
  → target axes **Perspective & reciprocity** / **Coexistence**.
- **"[Setzung] we posit a quantity I_µν"** reifies "information" into a countable
  source-substance. Whether information is a *bounded thing* that can be a
  *source* is a categorial assumption → axis **Object boundaries**.
- *(Side note — evidence:)* the paper's own tags [etabliert]/[Setzung]/[folgt]/
  [offen] are a **hand-built evidentiality system** — the author does by hand
  what Tuyuca grammar forces. The epistemic axis is already well served; the
  blind spot is the *direction* ("source"), not the sourcing.

## 2 · Contrast selection

Grounded lenses (with `focus`), German/English excluded:

- **Ancient Greek** — *"Geometric shape of an action"* (middle voice / aorist)
- **Georgian** — *"The human as a resonance space, not a causer"* (inversion)
- **Classical Chinese** — *"Pure relational constellation in the now"* (Coexistence)
- **Yucatec Maya** — *"Material instead of count"* (classifier; Object boundaries)
- **Chiasmus** — *"Inversion of priorities" (A-B:B-A)*; **Word ban**

## 3 · Reformulations

Core sentence: *"information is the source of geometry"* / the field equation.

- **Chiasmus (A-B : B-A):** "Information curves the geometry, the geometry counts
  the information." → *reveals:* the "=" runs **both ways** — G_µν *is*
  κℏω I_µν; calling one side the "source" is a reading, not a feature. The
  source→geometry rank dissolves.
- **Ancient Greek / middle voice:** "it curves-itself — information and geometry
  both partaking in the event." Curving as a middle-voice event: the subject is
  the *arena*, neither doer nor done-to. → *reveals:* "source" (active
  nominative) is grammatical scaffolding; the physics is a self-standing relation
  in which both terms participate.
- **Classical Chinese / parallel constellation:** "資訊 — 幾何 — 一體"
  ("information — geometry — one body"). No source, no arrow, no tense. →
  *reveals:* a **synchronic identity in the now**, not an emergence-over-time;
  "emergent" smuggles in a genealogy.
- **Yucatec Maya / classifier (material vs. count):** "information" is not a
  countable source-object; only the [Setzung] carves *one* density I_µν out of
  unbounded informational stuff. → *reveals:* the reification is an
  **individuation choice** — exactly what the author marks [Setzung] is the
  moment "information" is cut into a countable source; the classifier makes the
  cut visible as an assumption, not a given.
- **Word ban** (drop "source", "emergent", "follows from", "→"): "in one: curvature,
  entropy density, the same expression; divergence-free." → *reveals:* stripped
  of origin-words, what remains is an **equality constraint plus a conservation
  law** (∇^µ I_µν = 0) — no ancestry required.

## 4 · Overarching revelation

The paper's stated program — "read scattered results as aspects of **one**
informational view" — *is* the genealogical gesture: giving everything (gravity,
time, topology) a common ancestor (information). Axiom I's physics (a bilateral
"=" plus a conservation law) supports a **coexistence / identity reading at least
as well** as a source / emergence reading. "Source" does metaphysical work the
"=" does not demand. Notably self-referential: the essay behind `lat` warns
against exactly this tendency — here the tool turns on a paper whose deepest
[Setzung] embodies it.

## 5 · Framing

No version is more correct; the equation is unchanged — and the author is honest
that positing I_µν "as a source" is a [Setzung]. The detour only shows: the
choice between "information *causes* geometry" and "information and geometry are
the *same* conserved balance" is left open by the field equation itself, and it
shifts what seems to need explaining (not "why does information cause geometry?"
but "why are the two the same balance?"). It also isolates **"source" as the
load-bearing genealogical word** — the single term where the metaphysics enters.
